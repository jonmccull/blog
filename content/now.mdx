## Diagnoseovergang

[Diagnoseovergang](https://diagnoseovergang.no/) ("Diagnosis transition") aims to help health workers in Norway navigate the migration from ICD-10 to ICD-11, which is yet to get an official translation. It maps what's changed in the WHO's new classification and provides some simple diagnostic questionnaires for coding mental health disorders. The blog post goes into more detail. [Check it out](/blog/diagnoseovergang)! ([open source](https://github.com/jonmccull/diagnoseovergang) 🚀)
